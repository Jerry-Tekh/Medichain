Get Started Learn More
📜

Immutable Registration

Register clinical trials with protocol snapshots that cannot be altered, ensuring the original hypothesis and endpoints remain intact throughout the study.

🔬

AI Integrity Analysis

Advanced LLM-based analysis detects outcome switching, p-hacking, sample size discrepancies, and other integrity risks in submitted results.

🔒

Bond Guarantees

Sponsors post integrity bonds that are held in escrow. Fraud findings trigger automatic bond slashing, while clean results release the bond.

💰

Transparent Appeals

Regulators can review suspected fraud findings through a structured appeal process, with all decisions recorded immutably on-chain.

🔐

Whistleblower Protection

Authorized users can flag concerns with evidence. All flags are timestamped and linked to trials, creating a permanent audit trail.

📈

Live Dashboard

Monitor all trials, integrity scores, verdicts, and flags in real-time through a comprehensive dashboard with drill-down detail views.

How It Works

1

Register Trial

Sponsors register trials with ClinicalTrials.gov URLs, hypotheses, endpoints, and post an integrity bond.

2

Submit Results

Submit publication URLs and preprints. The system fetches and analyzes documents for integrity risks.

3

AI Analysis

LLM validators independently assess results, flagging concerns like outcome switching or p-hacking.

4

Appeal & Resolve

Regulators review flagged trials. Appeals result in bond release or slashing, finalizing the trial status.

5

Monitor Dashboard

Track all trials, scores, verdicts, and flags through the live integrity dashboard.
